# The AI Act's dates, as amended

Every application date of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (the AI Act) as it stands after Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (the Digital Omnibus on AI, in force 27 July 2026), one row per date, with the provision that fixes it.

## Files

- `dates.csv` and `dates.json`: 14 dates, sorted.

## Sources

- Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Article 113 and Article 111, Official Journal L, 12 July 2024.
- Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, Official Journal 2026-07-24, in force 2026-07-27: the high-risk dates moved to 2027-12-02 (Annex III) and 2028-08-02 (Annex I).

The dates are typed nowhere else: the site and the product read this same table. Last verified: 2026-09-12.
